# Exercise catalog attribution

The structured exercise data and Spanish instruction translations are derived
from [hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset](https://github.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset)
and are used under its MIT license.

The GIF and thumbnail URLs point to media by Gym Visual. Every use must retain:

> © Gym visual — https://gymvisual.com/

The media is not bundled in the Android application. It is loaded from the
dataset URLs stored in `exercise_catalog.gif_url` and `exercise_catalog.image_url`.
Reuse of the media remains subject to Gym Visual's terms and may require a
separate license.
